# taeguk-verify-measurement

Fix event unit, eligible population, observation boundary, time window, units and query version. Test known good, bad, unknown, duplicate, boundary and missing inputs. Use tools for exact calculations. Preserve raw inputs and uncertainty. Classify data quality separately from target judgment. Confirmed violations remain reportable even with partial evidence.

When this measurement check belongs to a shared verification plan, cite its exact version and check ID, approved oracle, artifact/data/environment version, method, scope, raw observations and gaps. Keep the result attached to that check; this skill does not require unrelated checks to define metrics or turn a metric result into overall completion.

Read [evidence packet](references/evidence-packet.md) when exchanging results or resuming work. Apply within the user's existing scope and authority; this skill does not authorize external effects or criteria changes.

Read [measurement evidence](references/measurement.md) when defining or interpreting operational and product metrics.
